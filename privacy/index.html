We keep it small

Privacy

Last updated October 12, 2026. Big AI Brain is designed to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your real name or email to use it.

What we store

  • A random feeder ID saved in your browser, so the brain can remember your nickname and your feeding track record. It isn't connected to who you are.
  • Your feeder nickname, the facts, jokes, questions and knowledge drops you choose to feed, and how you said you know them. Approved meals may be shown publicly with your nickname as credit.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address, used only to stop spam and abuse. We can't turn it back into your address.
  • Thumbs-up and thumbs-down ratings, meaning which answer got rated and how.
  • A chat counter. We count how many chats happen, but the messages you type in the chat are not saved.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The brain blocks personal info like names, addresses, phone numbers and emails if anyone tries to type them.

Where it lives

The brain and its data run on our own server. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show a Better Business Bureau seal image from the BBB, which means those services can see a basic request from your browser.

Removing your stuff

Want something you fed removed? Email us with your feeder nickname and what you fed, and we'll take it out of the brain's memory and future study sessions.
