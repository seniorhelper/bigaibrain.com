The evolution log

Watch it grow

Every time Big AI Brain gets an upgrade, we add a snapshot here: what it looks like, what's inside, what it can do. The stage names come from the brain itself; it has to earn each one with a better report card and enough approved meals.

Live right now: the neuron count and connection count on the homepage update as the brain grows. Each new stage adds neurons using function-preserving widening.
Day 1 · 🦠 Single Cell · v1.1 · 2026-10-12

Day one: born goofy

What it looks like: A smooth pink brain with googly eyes, buck teeth, blushing cheeks and one sprout on top. Eyes follow your finger. Mouth opens when you tap it.

What's inside: 384 neurons, about 483,000 connections, all random noise at birth. Two hidden layers. Reads 32 characters at a time and guesses the next one.

What it can do:

  • 171 clean jokes and 30 knock-knocks, both directions
  • Math that shows its work, plus a leveling kids quiz
  • 76 facts and 18 mini lessons
  • Memory lobe: approved meals answer instantly, credited to the feeder
  • Under the hood: explains its own code with pictures
  • Five-layer safety lobe and a rewind button
  • Grows new neurons when it earns a stage (function-preserving widening)

Snapshot coming once the new look settles. Tap the brain on the homepage to see it live.
Day 3 · 🫧 Amoeba · v1.3 · 2026-10-14

Day three: spinach, chlorella and a library card

What it looks like: Little muscles, two more sprouts, and a stretch when it grows. New things to say when you tap it.

What's inside: 584 neurons (was 480), growing 8 per meal from a person, ceiling 4,096. A Library lobe of openly licensed knowledge, credited by source. Nightly backups and a failure alarm.

What it can do:

  • Grows neurons the moment a meal is approved (function-preserving widening)
  • Learns from the Library lobe: Simple Wikipedia summaries and filtered human Q&A, CC BY-SA
  • Takes knowledge drops up to 4,000 characters, 10 a day
  • Keeps any one feeder under a quarter of the night's study
  • Emails its keeper if a study session fails
Day 14 · 🐟 Fish · v1.4 · night of 2026-10-24

Day fourteen: a Fish, and the new brain starts showing through

What it looks like: Still the goofy pink brain, now with glowing neurons. In patches, a dark high-tech brain glitches through: one glowing dot for every real neuron, signals racing between them. The patches spread as the neuron count really grows (12% of the way to the 4,096 max tonight). Swipe it and the dots scatter, change color and snap back.

What's inside: 946 neurons and about 1.7 million connections (it was 584 on day 3). 25 meals from people and 1,705 library cards. Report card 2.26 (it was 2.47 on day 3; lower is smarter). Earning the Fish stage grew a 96-neuron bonus on top of the 8 per meal.

What it can do now:

  • Eats twice a day, at 7am and 7pm Denver time: new Simple Wikipedia topics linked to what it already knows, plus OpenAssistant conversations to learn how people talk
  • Studies every night at 10pm Denver time
  • Stopped wrongly blocking everyday words like "spicy" and "spices"
  • Emails its keeper when meals have waited more than 12 hours
  • Its keeper can feed long text in parts through the Big spoon
  • Has a mission for its future home: it only grows bigger after its clean energy grows first (see the mission page)

The full path

  1. 🦠 Single Cell: born. Wired lobes work, the growing brain babbles.
  2. 🫧 Amoeba: letters come in groups. Unlocks after the first study session.
  3. 🐟 Fish: word-shaped babble, spaces in the right places. Needs 25 approved meals.
  4. 🔥 Caveman: real words, caveman grammar. 100 meals.
  5. 🧸 Toddler: short sentences that mostly make sense. 300 meals.
  6. 🎒 Kid: answers simple questions in its own words. 1,000 meals.
  7. 🛹 Teen: holds a real little conversation. 3,000 meals.
  8. 🎓 Graduate: smart enough to start helping with real work. 10,000 meals, and time for a bigger brain.
