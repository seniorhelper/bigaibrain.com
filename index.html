Our mission

Humanity's knowledge, given, not taken.

Most AI is built by scraping the world's words without asking. Big AI Brain is built the other way around: people choose to teach it. Every meal is given on purpose, credited to the person who gave it, and checked by a human before the brain studies it.

We believe every human is a genius in their own vertical. The plumber, the nurse, the beekeeper, the kid who knows every dinosaur. Bring your piece. Together it adds up to something no one company could build alone, and the world that raised it gets a say in where it goes.

Read the full mission Join the community at allofus.one

The ground rules

  • Feed what you made or know firsthand. Your trade, your experience, your own words.
  • Public knowledge is welcome when you write it in your own words: history, open science, common facts.
  • Never paste someone else's work. Not a scholar's paper, a song, a book, an article. If they didn't give it, we don't take it.
  • Humans check every meal before the brain studies it, and anyone can ask for their contribution to be removed.
  • Use it for good. By many estimates, tens of thousands of people die of hunger-related causes every single day. We think tools like this, raised by everyone, should help solve problems like that.

    Questions

    FAQ

    What is Big AI Brain?

    Big AI Brain is a baby AI built completely from scratch, with no outside AI company or API behind it. It starts out knowing almost nothing, and the public helps raise it by feeding it clean facts, jokes and questions. Every night it studies what it was fed and gets a little smarter.

    How does Big AI Brain learn?

    People feed it facts, jokes and questions with answers. Everything goes through automatic safety filters, then a human brain checker approves it. Approved items go into its memory right away, and its growing neural network studies them during a nightly study session.

    How do you keep bad stuff out of the brain?

    Five layers: an instant filter that blocks curse words, adult content and look-alike spellings; a block on links, emails, phone numbers and addresses; rate limits and spam detection; a human approval queue before anything is studied; and saved brain versions, so the brain can be rewound to an earlier day if anything weird ever gets in.

    Can kids use Big AI Brain?

    Yes. Kids can chat, hear jokes, take the math quiz and learn fun facts without signing up or sharing anything. Feeding the brain new material is for ages 13 and up.

    Why doesn't Big AI Brain know what a rock is?

    Because nobody has taught it yet! It only knows what it was born with and what people feed it. That's the whole fun: you help it learn.

    What does "given, not taken" mean?

    Most AI systems learn from text collected across the internet without asking the people who wrote it. Big AI Brain only learns from what people choose to feed it, with credit to the feeder and a human check before anything is studied. You can also ask for your contribution to be removed at any time.

    Can I upload documents or files?

    Not yet. Files are the easiest way for copied or junk material to sneak in. Instead, use a Knowledge Drop: up to 2,000 characters of your own writing on one topic. Every drop is read by a human before the brain studies it.

    Does the brain really grow new neurons?

    Yes. Each time it earns a new stage, it adds neurons using a technique called function-preserving widening: new neurons start as copies of existing ones, so nothing is forgotten, then they learn their own jobs during the next study session. The neuron count on the homepage is live.

    Do I need an account to feed the brain?

    No. Just pick a fun nickname. Feeders who get approved often become trusted feeders, and their clean submissions skip the line.
